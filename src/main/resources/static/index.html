Terms & Conditions

CampUs Student Community Guidelines & Code of Conduct
🏛️
Verified University Platform

Effective for all students, staff, and faculty on the CampUs network.

1
Acceptance & Campus Membership

By creating an account or posting on CampUs, you confirm that you are an active student, faculty, or staff member of the university network and agree to follow university discipline rules.

2
Authenticity of Lost & Found Reports

All items reported as Lost or Found must be genuine. Submitting false reports, prank posts, deceptive photos, or claiming property belonging to another student is strictly forbidden and subject to administrative disciplinary action.

3
Mandatory Ownership Verification

Before handing over a found item, finders must require claimants to show verifiable proof (e.g., student ID verification, unlocking the phone / laptop screen, matching unique stickers or serial marks, or describing exact pouch contents).

4
Safe Public Campus Handoffs

Physical exchanges of recovered items must take place in well-lit, central campus public areas (such as Central Library Reception, Security Guard Gate, Student Center, or Campus Department Helpdesks) during normal daytime hours.

5
Community Chat & Comment Conduct

CampUs maintains zero tolerance for cyber-bullying, harassment, abusive language, inappropriate photo uploads, spamming, unauthorized commercial advertisements, or solicitation in chat or comments.

6
Privacy & Contact Information Protection

Phone numbers, course details, and names shared on reports are strictly for returning lost items. Scraping, harvesting, or misusing student contact data for unsolicited outreach is a violation of user privacy and university regulations.

7
30-Day Post Retention & Permanent Wipeout

Deleted posts are preserved in 'Recently Deleted' for 30 calendar days to protect against accidental deletion. After 30 days, reports and associated media are permanently purged from the system.

8
Limitation of Liability

CampUs provides the communication tools to help peers connect. The university and platform developers do not hold custodial possession of items and are not liable for unrecovered belongings or interpersonal disputes.

Help & Support

Guides, FAQs & Campus Emergency Helpdesk
Frequently Asked Questions
How do I report an item I lost or found?

Tap the ＋ Report Item button in the top navigation or bottom bar. Select Lost or Found, enter a clear title, select the item category, campus location (e.g. Block 3, Canteen, Library), and upload a photo if available.

How do I mark an item as Returned?

Go to the 3-line menu → My Reports. Click Mark Returned ✨ on your post. This lets the whole campus know the item has been safely returned to its owner.

Can I restore a post I deleted accidentally?

Yes! Every deleted post is safely stored in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Open the 3-line menu → Your Activity → Recently Deleted tab, and tap Restore 🔄.

How do I share a post on WhatsApp or Instagram?

Every post card has a Share button at the bottom. Tap it to copy the link or share directly to your campus groups.

What should I do if I find high-value items (laptops, gold, purse)?

We recommend creating a Found report on CampUs, but depositing the physical item immediately with the Central Campus Security Office for secure safekeeping.
